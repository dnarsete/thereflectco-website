Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, plainly answered.

If your question isn't here, send us a note at support@thereflectco.com or through the contact form.

What does The Reflect Co. do?

The Reflect Co. is a proactive performance company. We build products that help people do better — for health, wellness, and longevity. We're the parent company of the Appose consumer brand and the LipTX line within it.

What is Appose?

Appose is our consumer brand — quiet-luxury, restrained, and built to last. Every Appose product is manufactured to the same proactive performance standard as everything The Reflect Co. makes. You can shop Appose directly at shopappose.com.

What is LipTX?

LipTX is a specialty line within Appose. It carries the same testing standard and packaging language as the rest of the Appose world, applied to a single focused category.

How do I become an authorized rep or distributor?

Send us a note through the Contact Us page with "Work with us" selected as the reason. Include your channel, geography, and the brands you currently carry. We're selective about territory expansion, but review every serious inquiry.

What is the Professional Portal?

The Professional Portal is the sign-in area for authorized representatives and distributors. It's a mobile-first CRM for order management, account notes, forecasting, and wholesale pricing. Access is invitation-only.

What are your wholesale terms?

Wholesale pricing is set centrally — reps cannot manually discount. Promotional pricing flows through approved promo codes only. Default shipping is a flat rate. Multi-jurisdiction tax is handled at order time. All sales are final; returns are accepted only for shipping damage.

Where are you located?

The Reflect Co. is based in Englewood, Colorado, at 3642 S. Jason Street. Business hours are Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm Mountain Time. You can reach us at support@thereflectco.com.

What does "Proactive Performance" mean?

It's our operating standard. Every formulation is batch-tested. Every claim is documented. Every shipment is traced. If we can't prove it, we don't say it. Proactive performance isn't a slogan — it's the gate every product has to pass before it ships.

Do you offer press or media inquiries?

Yes. Submit press requests through the Contact Us page with "Press" selected as the reason, or email support@thereflectco.com directly. We'll route media inquiries to the right person.
