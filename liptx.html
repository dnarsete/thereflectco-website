The Science

A formulation built,
not assembled.

LipTX was designed from the intended outcome backward — a lip treatment that performs measurably across the metrics that matter: hydration, barrier support, appearance, and comfort. Every ingredient earned its place by contribution, not by story.

01

Formulated to a defined benefit

Every input in the formula corresponds to a measured effect. If an ingredient doesn't contribute to hydration, barrier repair, or wear, it isn't in the bottle.

02

Batch-tested against the claim

Each production run is tested against the same benchmarks the formulation was designed to hit. If the batch doesn't match spec, the batch doesn't ship.

03

Independent-lab verified

Formulation testing is performed by third-party labs — the same labs that test the products of much larger houses. We publish the standards; the labs sign the results.

Clinical Substantiation

The Repeat Insult Patch Test is a controlled dermatological study measuring cumulative skin response. It is not an efficacy test; it establishes that a formulation is safe for repeated daily use across a diverse skin cohort. Additional efficacy studies are in planning.

Formulation

Four actives.
One quiet result.

Each active earned its place by contribution — barrier, brightness, or finish. Nothing is in the bottle to tell a story.

Defense

Marine antioxidant complex

An ocean-sourced antioxidant blend chosen to protect the lip surface against oxidative wear from environment and daily habit.

Nourish

Sea buckthorn

A cold-pressed botanical oil dense with omegas and carotenoids. Comfort you notice; a color you don't.

Brighten

THD vitamin C

Tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate — a stable, oil-soluble form of vitamin C that works into the formulation without the fragility of ascorbic acid.

Finish

Liquid-crystal cholesteryl

A liquid-crystal glow finish — reads as light on the surface, not as a coating. Lightly flavored; no fragrance.

The Ritual

Three steps.
Twice a day.

  1. 01

    Apply

    A measured amount to clean lips, morning and evening.

  2. 02

    Settle

    Wait ninety seconds. The formulation is designed to absorb, not to sit.

  3. 03

    Refresh

    Reapply as needed through the day. It doesn't fight water, food, or wear — it recovers between them.
